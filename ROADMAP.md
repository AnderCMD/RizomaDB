# Roadmap

English · [Español](ROADMAP.es.md)

The honest status of what is verified and what is not is in the [acceptance checklist](docs/release/v1-checklist.md).

## After 1.0.0
- Publish to Open VSX (blocked for now by an account problem).
- Testing by other people and on real cloud accounts (see [Help test RizomaDB](docs/guides/help-test.md)).
- An independent accessibility audit.
- Run the per-platform packages of the release workflow on every platform (only linux-x64 and darwin-arm64 have been run).

## Not planned
- Redshift, Snowflake and BigQuery: they cannot be tested without accounts, and the project does not claim support it has not tested.
- A JDBC/ODBC bridge and Level 3 engines.
