# Governance

English · [Español](GOVERNANCE.es.md) · [Português (Brasil)](GOVERNANCE.pt-br.md) · [Français](GOVERNANCE.fr.md)

The project is maintained by its authors (a benevolent dictator model). Technical decisions are recorded as ADRs in [docs/adr](docs/adr) (written in Spanish, the project's working language). No feature will ever be locked behind a payment or an account. Funding, if any, is optional sponsorship.
