# Governance

English · [Español](GOVERNANCE.es.md)

The project is maintained by its authors (a benevolent dictator model). Technical decisions are made in the open, in issues and pull requests. No feature will ever be locked behind a payment or an account. Funding, if any, is optional sponsorship.
